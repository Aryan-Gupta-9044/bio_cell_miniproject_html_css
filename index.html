INTRODUCTION

What is a Cell?

A cell is the basic structural and functional unit of life. Every living organism is made up of one or more cells. Cells contain specialized structures called organelles that perform different functions necessary for survival.

🧱

Basic Unit of Life

Cells form the fundamental building blocks of all living organisms.

⚙️

Specialized Functions

Different organelles perform specialized tasks such as energy production, protein synthesis and waste removal.

🧬

Genetic Information

DNA stores genetic instructions that control cellular activities and inheritance.

🔄

Continuous Activity

Cells constantly perform chemical reactions required for growth, repair and maintaining life.

CELL TYPES

Animal Cell vs Plant Cell

Animal and plant cells are both eukaryotic cells, but they have several structural differences that help them perform their specific biological functions.

N
EUKARYOTIC

Animal Cell

Animal cells are surrounded by a cell membrane and contain a nucleus and membrane-bound organelles. They do not have a cell wall or chloroplasts.
