CELL TYPES

Understanding Different Cells

Cells are the basic structural and functional units of life. Although all cells share some common characteristics, plant and animal cells have important structural differences.

ANIMAL CELL

Animal Cell

Animal cells are eukaryotic cells containing a membrane-bound nucleus and specialized organelles. They do not have a cell wall or chloroplasts.

  • ✓ Has a cell membrane
  • ✓ Contains nucleus
  • ✓ Contains mitochondria
  • ✓ Has centrioles
PLANT CELL

Plant Cell

Plant cells are eukaryotic cells surrounded by a rigid cell wall. They contain chloroplasts that allow plants to perform photosynthesis.

  • ✓ Cell wall
  • ✓ Chloroplasts
  • ✓ Large central vacuole
  • ✓ Nucleus and mitochondria
